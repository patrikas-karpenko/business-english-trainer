Unit 1: Plan Before You Type

Part 1 of 7

1. New Vocabulary

Review these words before we begin.

  • subject: what an email is about
  • reader: the person who reads your email
  • purpose: the reason you write; what you want
  • deadline: the last day or time to do something

2. Vocabulary Check

1. Who is the of this email?
2. The of this email is to ask for a new printer.

3. Read & Compare

Email A

Subject: Hello

Hi Anna, how are you? The weather is terrible this week! On Monday I had a meeting with Lukas... I think it is a good idea for us. It is not very cheap. What do you think?
Email B

Subject: Budget request – Milan trade fair, 12–14 March

Dear Anna, I’m writing to ask for a budget for the Home & Garden trade fair... The total cost is €2,400. Could you approve the budget by Friday, 5 February?

4. Extracting Key Data

Hello Tomas,

We have finalized the logistics for the Home & Garden trade fair. The total cost is €2,400, which includes the stand and travel. The shipping containers will arrive on the 26th of October, 2026. Please ensure payment is approved.
